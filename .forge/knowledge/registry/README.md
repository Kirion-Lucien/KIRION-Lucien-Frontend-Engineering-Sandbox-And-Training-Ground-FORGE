# SOURCE REGISTRY

sources.json is the machine-readable registry of qualified source records.

## W01 initial state

The registry is intentionally empty. W01 performs no pilot ingestion and seeds no remembered sites, libraries, repositories, frameworks, design systems, or visual references.

This prevents appearance of maturity from replacing evidence.

## Future mutation law

A future authorized acquisition run may add records only after K0-K2 qualification under ../../protocols/KNOWLEDGE_ACQUISITION.md and ../../protocols/SOURCE_EVALUATION.md.

Each future source entry must conform to ../schemas/source-record.schema.json and retain provenance, version/recency, authority reason, licensing state, and storage policy.

Registry inclusion means “qualified source record exists.” It does not mean the source or its claims are accepted Forge guidance.
